# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct.

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness.
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes.
- Prioritizing what is best for the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Trolling, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Harassment or discrimination in any form.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards
of acceptable behavior and will take fair corrective action in response to any
unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces and whenever an individual
is officially representing the project.

## Reporting

Report incidents to the maintainers via the security contact listed in
SECURITY.md. Reports will be handled promptly and confidentially.
